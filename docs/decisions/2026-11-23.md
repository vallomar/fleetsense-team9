## Observed patterns and anomaly handling

The analysis uses all twelve source files, but only accepted records. Findings are descriptive and do not explain causes.

- Hour: 18:00 has the highest average accepted pickups; 04:00 is the trough. Retain these observations. Do not translate them into patient-transfer staffing advice.
- Weekday: Thursday is the highest average per calendar occurrence, Sunday the lowest. Divide by the actual 52/53 weekday occurrences rather than comparing raw sums.
- Month: October has about 106,498 accepted trips/day; August about 82,660. The summer trough is retained, not removed as an outlier. Month-specific quarantine rates differ, so do not attribute this solely to demand or holidays.

Retain zero-count zones in the bottom ranking; show zone IDs because the lookup contains repeated display names. IDs 264/265 are not named geographic zones and are excluded only from these rankings, not totals.
